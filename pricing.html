Pricing and the beta

Scratchwork is an invite-only beta. No prices are set yet, so none are listed here.

What the beta is

  • The app as it is today, on Android tablets and phones and on Windows. Some parts are rough.
  • Nothing in the beta can charge you. Payments are switched off in the beta build.
  • AI features need an account and have daily limits.
  • You can send a bug report or a request from inside the app with Send feedback.

What is planned

The plan is to charge for the account, and to sell AI plans on top for the features that call an AI model, like practice tests, lessons and the tutor. Those cost real money every time they run, which is why they will be a paid part. The prices are not final. They will be posted on this page before anything is charged.

How to get an invite

  1. Email ntblair417+scratchwork@gmail.com. The link fills in a subject and two lines to complete.
  2. Say which device you will use: Android tablet, Android phone or Windows.
  3. I reply with how to install it.

That is the whole process. There is no form on this site and I do not need your name, school or phone number to start.
