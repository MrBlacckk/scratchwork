Questions and answers

The short version of what people ask first. If yours is not here, ask me.

How do I get in?

Scratchwork is an invite-only beta. Email me with the device you will use and I will reply with how to install it.

What does it cost?

No prices are set yet, and nothing in the beta can charge you. The plan is a paid account plus AI plans. See pricing and the beta.

Which devices does it run on?

Android tablets and phones, and Windows PCs. iOS is planned. I build and test mostly on a Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE with its pen, so that is the best-tested device.

Do I need an account?

Yes, during the beta. An account needs an invite code. It gives you sync between devices and the AI features. AI features have daily limits.

How does the math checking work?

You write a problem by hand. The app reads your handwriting, then checks each line against the one before it and marks the first line that went wrong, with a fix. Steps it can decide on your device are checked there without AI. Harder ones go to an AI model.

It can be wrong, and it can misread handwriting. Tap a part to correct what it read, and use Report this answer on a bad AI answer.

Where do my notes go?

They stay on your device unless you sign in. When you are signed in, your notebooks and files sync to our host, Supabase, in the United States. When you use an AI feature, the request goes through our server to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer. I do not sell your data, there are no ads, and your notes are not used to train AI models. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Does it work with Blackboard, WebAssign and WeBWorK?

Yes. You sign in on your school's own page inside the app, and Scratchwork reads due dates, files and homework from it. It never submits, saves or starts anything, and it does not store your school password. I built and tested this against one university first, so another school's sign-in may behave differently.

Is it made by Blackboard, my school or my textbook company?

No. Scratchwork is made by one person and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blackboard, WebAssign, WeBWorK or any school.

Can I use it on graded work?

Use it to practice and understand. Check your course's rules before using any tool on graded work. That is your call and your instructor's.

Can I get my notes out?

Yes. Settings, Account, Export my data saves every notebook, page, picture and recording in one .zip file. A single notebook exports as PDF, text or LaTeX.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. Or email me. The steps and what gets removed are on the delete account page.

Who can use it?

You must be 18 or older. Scratchwork is made for university students.

I found a bug or I want a feature.

Use Send feedback in Settings, or in a folder's More menu. Or email me.
