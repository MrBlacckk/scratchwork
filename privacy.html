Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 Controller: Nolan Blair, Grand Forks, North Dakota, United States, ntblair417+scratchwork@gmail.com

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what Scratchwork (the "App") collects, where it is stored, who receives it, and the choices you have. It covers the Android, iOS and iPadOS, and Windows apps and its website (https://mrblacckk.github.io/scratchwork/). The App is in an invite-only beta: creating an account needs an invite code. The App is for people 18 and older (see section 8).

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your notes or your AI requests to train AI models. Anthropic does not train its models on requests sent through its commercial API, which is how the App reaches it.

Not collected today: we do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs or crash-reporting services we run. Error details, including the technical trace of a crash, reach us only in feedback you choose to send (see the feedback row above).

Google ML Kit: the App uses Google ML Kit on device for ink recognition and document scanning. Google's own handling of data in ML Kit is set by Google's ML Kit terms, not by us. Google says ML Kit may send it diagnostics: device information, the app version, an installation identifier, and performance and error codes, encrypted in transit. It does not send your handwriting or scans.

On-device model download: the App downloads its on-device AI model (about 2.4 GB) from huggingface.co. That download shows your IP address to Hugging Face. The App asks before it starts the download.

Finding your school: to find your school's site, the App sends the words you type to Instructure's public school search (Canvas only) and to Wikidata's public search, then checks the likely web addresses. These are plain requests with no sign-in. They show those words and your IP address to those services, and your IP address to the sites it checks.

Sideload updates: copies of the App installed outside the Play Store check our update service for new builds and download them from GitHub, which sees your IP address. The Play build does not do this: it has no updater and does not ask for permission to install apps.

3. Student records

The App is for students to use for their own study. It is not a school system, and it does not receive records from a school's gradebook. We have no agreement with any school. If a school or instructor provides the App to you, the school's own policies also apply.

5. How long we keep data

  • Synced data stays while your account is open. A notebook or page you delete stays on our backend, marked deleted, until your account is deleted, so your other devices learn of the delete.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your synced data and your sign-in from our backend at once (in-app; live since Build 124). Backup copies kept by our host expire within 30 days.
  • Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. On Android, the system backup may copy your notes to your Google account; on iPhone and iPad, to iCloud, if you have device backup on.
  • Our host keeps sign-in logs (times and IP addresses) for its own retention period. Deleting your account does not remove them.
  • Your row in the invite list is deleted with your account.
  • AI answer reports are deleted when your account is deleted (the table links to your account and is removed with it).
  • Feedback you send is deleted when your account is deleted, the same way.
  • Your plan, credit balance and credit log are deleted with your account. Google Play, Stripe and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records as the law requires (for example, for tax).
  • We keep no log of AI request or reply content. Daily AI counts and cost totals are kept while your account is open and are deleted with it.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our backend is limited by row-level security, and the AI service key is kept only on our server. Notebooks on your device are stored in a plain (unencrypted) local database; the App lock controls access inside the App, not the device's storage.

If we learn of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings > Account > Export my data gives you a zip of your data, including study tables as JSON. Import restores it.
  • Delete: in the App under Settings > Account > Delete account (type DELETE). This removes your synced rows, your files in our storage and your sign-in. The device keeps your notes unowned unless you tick the box to wipe them. Web deletion page: https://mrblacckk.github.io/scratchwork/delete-account.html. Support: ntblair417+scratchwork@gmail.com.
  • Correct: edit your data in the App.
  • AI consent and stopping AI use: the App asks your permission, once, before it sends anything to Anthropic's Claude. Until you tap Allow, no AI request leaves your device. Change your mind any time with the switch in Settings > AI features, or simply do not use the AI features. The rest of the App, including reading handwriting on your device, works without them.
  • California (CCPA) and EU/UK (GDPR) rights: you may ask us what we hold, correct it, delete it, or object to processing. We will answer within 45 days. Email ntblair417+scratchwork@gmail.com. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we will not treat you worse for using these rights. Someone you authorize may make a request for you; we may ask you to confirm it is you.
  • EU/UK users, our legal grounds: to run your account and sync (a contract with you); to send your request to Anthropic (your consent, given on the Allow screen and withdrawn in Settings > AI features); to keep the service safe, count usage and review reported answers (our legitimate interest). You may complain to your local data protection authority. Section 9 explains the transfer to the United States.

8. Children

The App is for people 18 and older, as the Terms say, and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and so none from anyone under 13. Sign-up asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. If you believe a person under 18 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Our servers are in the United States (Oregon). If you use the App from outside the United States, your data is transferred there and handled as this policy describes.
