Delete your Scratchwork account

In the app

  1. Open Settings, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete next to Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm. Tick Also remove the notes on this device to clear the device too.

Without the app

Email ntblair417+scratchwork@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". Include the email address the account uses (if you write from another one, we reply to that address to confirm it is yours first). Nothing else is needed: no password. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Want a copy first? In the app, Settings, Account, Export my data saves every notebook, page, picture and recording in one .zip file. The Delete account dialog offers it too.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name and password.
  • Everything synced to it: folders, notebooks, pages, ink, text boxes and other page objects, and the files stored with them (pictures, PDFs, recordings).
  • Your AI usage and spend counts.
  • Any reports you sent about AI answers (they are linked to your account).
  • Any feedback you sent from the app.

Deletion in the app is immediate and cannot be undone. From the moment it starts, nobody can sign in to the account, and other devices signed in to it stop syncing. Backup copies kept by our host expire within 30 days. Notes still on a device stay there unless you tick the box above or uninstall the app.
